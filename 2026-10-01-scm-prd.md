# Product Requirements Document

## SCM Channel PO, Shipment and Warehouse Inventory Management System

**Version:** 1.0  
**Date:** 1 October 2026  
**Status:** Draft for product and engineering review  
**Primary users:** SCM Team Members, SCM Managers, Administrators and QC Users  
**Source of truth:** Approved operational workflow supplied by the business  
**Supporting reference:** Novel SCM & Quality Control Platform Specification v2.0

---

## 1. Product summary

The product is a workflow-first SCM system for managing marketplace purchase orders from receipt through delivery and invoice upload.

It replaces fragmented Excel sheets, email forwarding, manually calculated warehouse stock and unstructured WhatsApp updates with one shared operational record.

The MVP covers:

1. PO PDF upload and data extraction.
2. PO review, acceptance and rejection.
3. Automatic warehouse assignment from the delivery destination.
4. Stock threshold protection and inventory reservation.
5. Appointment letter upload and matching.
6. Multi-truck shipment planning.
7. Manual shipment-status updates.
8. Proof-of-delivery tracking.
9. POD-based inventory deduction.
10. Upload of one externally generated invoice per PO.
11. Alerts, work queues, permissions and a complete audit log.

The system does not manage production, generate invoices or book marketplace appointments.

---

## 2. Problem statement

The SCM team currently receives POs from Amazon/RK World, Flipkart and other platforms. PO information, appointment documents, warehouse stock, shipment splits, driver updates, PODs and invoices are managed across PDFs, Excel files, email and WhatsApp.

This creates the following problems:

- PO details are repeatedly copied by hand.
- Appointment deadlines can be missed.
- Warehouse selection depends on people remembering destination rules.
- The same stock can be promised to more than one PO.
- Minimum protected stock can be consumed accidentally.
- One PO split across several trucks is difficult to reconcile.
- Shipment updates are not stored in one place.
- Inventory is not consistently separated into available, blocked and in-transit stock.
- PODs and invoices are difficult to trace back to the correct PO.
- It is difficult to determine who made a change and why.

---

## 3. Product goal

Create one reliable system where the SCM team can process each PO from PDF upload to final invoice while the system automatically protects inventory, applies warehouse-routing rules, tracks partial shipments and records every important action.

### 3.1 Success outcomes

The product is successful when:

- The SCM team no longer needs an Excel tracker to operate the workflow.
- Every PO, appointment, shipment, POD and invoice can be found from one PO screen.
- Accepted quantities are blocked without using protected threshold stock.
- No stock is reserved for two POs at the same time.
- Every dispatch and delivery produces a correct inventory movement.
- Every PO is automatically assigned to the correct source warehouse.
- Every change can be traced to a user and timestamp.
- Pending work and exceptions are visible without checking email or WhatsApp history.

## 5. Users and permissions

### 5.1 SCM Team Member

Can:

- Upload PO and appointment PDFs.
- Review and correct extracted PO information.
- Accept or reject POs.
- Add inventory through controlled stock-addition transactions.
- Create shipments and allocate SKU quantities.
- Record vehicle, driver, cost and shipment updates.
- Upload PODs.
- Confirm delivered quantities.
- Upload the final invoice.
- Resolve normal operational exceptions.

Cannot:

- Change user permissions.
- Override an automatically assigned warehouse.
- Change protected stock thresholds.
- Approve a short close.
- Delete historical records or inventory movements.

### 5.2 SCM Manager

Has all SCM Team Member permissions and can:

- Override the assigned warehouse with a mandatory reason.
- Approve a short close.
- Configure or change SKU-warehouse thresholds.
- Resolve restricted exceptions.
- Reopen an operational record when permitted.

### 5.3 Administrator

Can:

- Manage users and roles.
- Manage platforms, warehouses and SKUs.
- Manage destination-to-warehouse mappings.
- Manage route estimates and system settings.
- Perform the same restricted actions as an SCM Manager.

An Administrator must not be able to alter or remove historical audit and inventory-movement records.

### 5.4 QC User

Has view-only access to:

- POs and PO lines.
- SKU and inventory information.
- Appointment letters.
- Shipments and shipment updates.
- PODs and delivery discrepancies.
- Uploaded invoices.
- Audit history.

A QC User cannot create, edit, approve, upload or delete any record.

## 7. Functional requirements

## 7.1 Platform management

The system shall allow Administrators to create and maintain platforms such as Amazon/RK World and Flipkart.

Each platform record shall support:

- Platform name and code.
- Active or inactive status.
- Expected PO-ID field or pattern.
- Expected appointment document format.
- Configurable rejection reasons.
- Configurable reminder timings.

## 7.2 PO PDF upload and extraction

### Requirements

1. An SCM Team Member shall upload a PO PDF and select the source platform.
2. The original file shall be stored without being overwritten.
3. The system shall extract, where present:
   - Platform.
   - PO ID.
   - PO date.
   - Appointment-booking deadline or PO expiry.
   - Delivery destination.
   - Fulfilment-centre name or code.
   - Vendor or customer name.
   - SKU.
   - Ordered quantity.
   - Unit price.
   - Line value and total PO value.
   - Required shelf life or expiry information.
   - Delivery window.
   - Other platform instructions.
4. Extracted values shall be shown beside the source PDF for review.
5. The user shall be able to correct extracted values before submitting the PO for review.
6. Required fields shall be validated before the PO can be accepted.
7. A platform and PO-ID combination shall be unique.
8. Re-uploading the same document shall not create another PO.
9. An unreadable PDF shall enter a document-review exception queue.
10. The system shall retain extraction confidence and the corrected final value for future diagnostics.

## 7.3 Automatic warehouse assignment

The system shall assign the source warehouse using the extracted platform and delivery destination.

### Destination mapping priority

The matching order shall be:

1. Exact platform and destination/FC code.
2. Exact platform and destination name.
3. City and state mapping.
4. No match, resulting in a Warehouse Mapping Required exception.

Each mapping shall contain:

- Platform.
- Destination name.
- Destination or FC code.
- City and state.
- Assigned source warehouse.
- Optional backup warehouse.
- Effective start date.
- Active status.

The assigned warehouse shall be visible before PO acceptance so the team can review allocatable stock.

Only an SCM Manager or Administrator may override the assigned warehouse. Every override requires a reason and audit entry.

The system shall never silently switch warehouses because of a shortage.

## 7.4 PO review, acceptance and rejection

### PO review statuses

- Uploaded.
- Extraction Review Required.
- Under Review.
- Accepted.
- Rejected.
- Cancelled.

### Acceptance requirements

Before acceptance, the system shall show:

- Extracted and verified PO information.
- Assigned source warehouse.
- Physical, blocked, threshold and allocatable quantities for every SKU.
- Shortage quantity, if any.
- Appointment-booking deadline.

When accepted:

1. The system shall create a reservation for the allocatable quantity.
2. The reserved quantity shall enter the Blocked state.
3. The stock shall no longer be available to another PO.
4. Any unfulfilled quantity shall remain recorded as a shortage.
5. An appointment task shall be created.
6. An automated SCM email shall be sent.
7. The acceptance user and timestamp shall be recorded.

When rejected:

1. A rejection reason shall be mandatory.
2. No inventory shall be blocked.
3. The rejection user and timestamp shall be recorded.

## 7.5 SCM notification

The acceptance email shall include:

- Platform and PO ID.
- Customer or vendor.
- Delivery destination.
- Assigned source warehouse.
- SKU and quantity breakdown.
- Allocated and shortage quantities.
- PO value.
- Appointment-booking deadline.
- Delivery window.
- Link to the PO.
- PO PDF attachment or secure document link.

Email failures shall create a visible exception and allow retry without repeating the stock reservation.

## 7.6 Appointment management

1. Appointment booking shall remain manual on the marketplace platform.
2. Each PO shall have one appointment letter.
3. The team shall upload the appointment PDF after the PO PDF.
4. The system shall extract the PO ID from the appointment PDF and automatically match it to the existing PO.
5. If automatic matching fails or produces more than one candidate, the user shall select the PO manually.
6. The system shall prevent the appointment letter from being linked to multiple POs.
7. Replacing an appointment letter shall require a reason and preserve the previous version in history.
8. Shipment planning shall remain locked until the appointment letter is present.

The appointment record shall contain:

- PO reference.
- Appointment ID.
- Booking date.
- Appointment date and time.
- Appointment location.
- Appointment status.
- Appointment PDF.
- Upload user and timestamp.

Appointment statuses:

- Pending Booking.
- Booked.
- Rescheduled.
- Missed.
- Completed.
- Cancelled.

The system shall alert the SCM team before the booking deadline and when an accepted PO has no appointment letter.

## 7.7 Inventory model

Inventory shall be maintained per SKU and warehouse.

### Quantities

- **Physical stock:** Stock recorded at the warehouse, excluding stock already dispatched.
- **Threshold stock:** Protected minimum that cannot be allocated to a PO.
- **Blocked stock:** Stock reserved for accepted POs but not dispatched.
- **Available stock:** Physical stock minus blocked stock.
- **Allocatable stock:** Physical stock minus blocked stock minus threshold stock.
- **In-transit stock:** Dispatched stock still owned by the company.
- **Delivered stock:** Quantity confirmed by POD and removed from company inventory.

```text
Available stock = Physical stock - Blocked stock

Allocatable stock =
max(0, Physical stock - Blocked stock - Threshold stock)
```

### Inventory rules

1. Threshold stock shall be configured per SKU per warehouse.
2. Threshold stock shall not be allocated through a normal PO acceptance or allocation action.
3. Threshold changes require SCM Manager or Administrator access.
4. A threshold change shall not retroactively cancel existing reservations.
5. If a threshold increase causes allocatable stock to become negative, the system shall show zero allocatable stock and create an exception.
6. Inventory additions shall be entered by the SCM team as stock-addition movements with a reason and reference.
7. Users shall not directly edit a calculated inventory balance.
8. Every inventory change shall create an immutable movement record.
9. Reversals shall be used to correct mistakes.
10. Concurrent acceptance attempts shall lock and recheck the affected SKU-warehouse balance before reservation.

### Inventory lifecycle

```text
PO accepted:       Allocatable -> Blocked
PO cancelled:      Blocked -> Allocatable
Truck dispatched:  Blocked -> In Transit
POD confirmed:     In Transit -> Delivered/out of company inventory
Delivery reversed: Delivered -> In Transit through a reversal transaction
```

## 7.8 Shortage handling

When allocatable stock is lower than the PO requirement, the system shall display:

- Required quantity.
- Physical stock.
- Blocked stock.
- Threshold stock.
- Allocatable stock.
- Shortage quantity.
- Stock at other warehouses for information only.

The system may reserve the allocatable quantity and shall record the remaining shortage.

The team may:

- Wait for a stock addition.
- Allocate the remaining quantity after new stock arrives.
- Request a warehouse override.
- Use a configured backup warehouse after approval.
- Split fulfilment across warehouses after approval.
- Reject or short-close the PO.

The MVP shall not plan or manage production for shortages.

## 7.9 Route estimates

Administrators shall maintain route estimates for each warehouse and destination pair.

Each route shall contain:

- Source warehouse.
- Destination.
- Estimated transit time.
- Estimated cost.
- Effective date.
- Active status.

The shipment shall store the estimate copied at creation time so later master changes do not alter historical plans.

Actual time and cost shall be captured separately.

## 7.10 Shipment planning

One PO may contain multiple SKUs and multiple shipments or trucks.

Each shipment shall contain:

- System-generated shipment number.
- PO reference.
- Appointment reference.
- Source warehouse.
- Destination.
- Vehicle or truck number.
- Vehicle size or capacity.
- Transporter.
- Driver name and phone number.
- Planned and actual dispatch date.
- Expected and actual arrival date.
- Estimated and actual cost.
- LR or transport document.
- SKU and planned quantity lines.
- Status and remarks.

### Shipment rules

1. Shipment planning requires an appointment letter.
2. A shipment may contain one or more PO SKUs.
3. A PO SKU may be split across multiple shipments.
4. Total planned quantity across active shipments shall not exceed the blocked quantity.
5. Dispatch quantity shall not exceed the shipment's planned quantity.
6. Cancelling an undispatched shipment shall release its shipment allocation but not the PO reservation unless the PO allocation is also cancelled.
7. Changing a dispatched shipment's SKU quantities shall require a reversal and correction transaction.

Shipment statuses:

- Planned.
- Vehicle Assigned.
- Loading.
- Dispatched.
- In Transit.
- Arrived at Destination.
- Unloading.
- Partially Delivered.
- Delivered.
- Delivery Issue.
- Returned.
- Cancelled.

## 7.11 Shipment updates

Driver updates received through WhatsApp shall be entered manually by the SCM team.

Each update shall contain:

- Shipment reference.
- Status.
- Event date and time.
- Current location.
- Notes.
- Optional image or document.
- Entry user and timestamp.

Updates shall appear as a chronological timeline. Editing an update shall preserve its earlier value in the audit history.

## 7.12 Proof of delivery

The system shall support one or more POD documents per PO. The default operational model is one POD per truck, but a POD may be linked to more than one shipment when the received document covers several trucks.

Each POD shall contain:

- PO reference and one or more shipment references.
- Delivery date and time.
- Dispatched quantity by SKU.
- Confirmed received quantity by SKU.
- Short quantity.
- Damaged or rejected quantity.
- Receiver name.
- POD image or PDF.
- Discrepancy reason and notes.
- Upload and confirmation user.

### POD rules

1. POD confirmation shall remove only the confirmed delivered quantity from in-transit company stock.
2. Short, damaged or rejected quantities shall remain in an unresolved delivery state until disposition is recorded.
3. A shipment shall not become Delivered while any dispatched quantity remains unresolved.
4. Re-uploading the same POD shall not repeat the inventory deduction.
5. A corrected POD shall use a controlled replacement and inventory reversal process.
6. The PO shall become Fully Delivered only when all required quantities are confirmed or an approved short close covers the remaining quantity.

## 7.13 Short close

A short close allows a PO to complete with less than its ordered quantity.

Requirements:

- Only an SCM Manager or Administrator may approve it.
- The remaining quantity and reason shall be recorded.
- Any related blocked stock shall be released.
- The approval shall appear in the audit log.
- Invoice upload may proceed after approval.

## 7.14 Invoice upload

1. The system shall not generate invoices.
2. The team shall create the invoice outside the SCM product.
3. Each PO shall have one invoice.
4. The invoice upload action shall remain disabled until:
   - The PO is fully delivered, or
   - An approved short close exists.
5. The invoice shall be linked to the PO, appointment, shipments and PODs.
6. The original invoice file shall be retained.

The invoice record shall contain:

- Invoice number.
- Invoice date.
- Invoice amount.
- Invoice PDF.
- Upload user and date.
- Sent date.
- Recipients.
- Sending status.
- Resend history.

The system may send the uploaded invoice through email, but it shall not calculate tax or invoice values.

## 7.15 PO closure

A PO may close only when:

- All shipments are delivered or resolved.
- The full PO quantity is delivered or short-closed.
- No delivery discrepancy remains open.
- The single invoice has been uploaded.

Closing shall be a recorded status transition. Closed records shall remain searchable and viewable.

## 10. Audit requirements

The audit log shall be append-only.

Each audit event shall record:

- User and role.
- Server timestamp.
- Action.
- Record type and record ID.
- Old value.
- New value.
- Reason, when required.
- Related PO, shipment, SKU or warehouse.
- Source screen or automated process.

Actions requiring explicit audit events include:

- PO upload, correction, acceptance, rejection, cancellation and closure.
- Warehouse automatic assignment and override.
- Stock addition, reservation, release, dispatch, delivery and reversal.
- Threshold change.
- Appointment upload or replacement.
- Shipment creation, change, cancellation and status update.
- POD upload, confirmation, replacement and discrepancy resolution.
- Short-close approval.
- Invoice upload, send and resend.
- User, role and master-data changes.

Audit records shall not be editable by any application role.

### 13.1 Key constraints

- Unique `platform_id + external_po_id`.
- Maximum one active appointment per PO.
- Maximum one invoice per PO.
- Shipment lines must reference SKUs present on the PO.
- Active shipment allocation cannot exceed blocked PO quantity.
- Confirmed POD quantity cannot exceed dispatched quantity.
- Combined confirmed POD quantities cannot exceed the outstanding dispatched quantity for each shipment line.
- Inventory movements are immutable.
- Audit events are immutable.

## 14. Non-functional requirements

### 14.1 Performance

- Normal pages shall load within 2 seconds at the 95th percentile under expected business load.
- Inventory reservation shall complete atomically and return a result within 2 seconds.
- Search results shall return within 3 seconds for five years of operational history.
- PDF extraction may run asynchronously but shall show a clear processing status.

### 14.2 Security

- All users shall authenticate.
- Permissions shall be checked on the server for every restricted action.
- Administrator and SCM Manager accounts shall support multi-factor authentication.
- Data shall be encrypted in transit and at rest.
- Documents shall be accessible only to authorised users.
- Server time shall be used for audit and inventory events.

### 14.3 Reliability

- Automated jobs shall use retries without duplicating business actions.
- Failed jobs shall enter a visible failure queue.
- Reservation, dispatch and POD operations shall be idempotent.
- Daily automated backups are required.
- A restore test shall succeed before production go-live.
- No business record shall be hard-deleted.

### 14.4 Document retention

POs, appointments, PODs, invoices and audit records shall be retained according to the company retention policy. Until a separate policy is approved, the application shall not automatically delete these records.

7. A threshold changed after stock is already reserved.
8. Two users accepting POs for the same last available stock at the same time.
9. An appointment PDF uploaded before the matching PO exists.
10. An appointment PDF whose PO ID cannot be extracted.
11. A shipment split across many trucks and SKUs.
12. A truck carrying only part of one SKU.
13. Multiple POD documents for one shipment.
14. One POD covering more than one truck.
15. A POD confirming less than the dispatched quantity.
16. Damaged or rejected quantity at delivery.
17. A truck returned without successful delivery.
18. A POD accidentally uploaded twice.
19. A corrected POD after inventory deduction.
20. A PO cancelled after stock was blocked.
21. A shipment cancelled after dispatch.
22. An invoice upload attempted before full delivery.
23. Email failure after PO acceptance.
24. A warehouse override made after shipment planning starts.

## 16. Acceptance criteria

1. A user can upload a supported PO PDF and review the extracted fields before acceptance.
2. The system rejects a duplicate platform and PO-ID combination.
3. The delivery destination automatically selects the configured source warehouse.
4. A missing warehouse mapping creates a visible exception and prevents automatic assignment.
5. Only an SCM Manager or Administrator can override the assigned warehouse, and the reason appears in the audit log.
6. The system calculates allocatable stock as physical minus blocked minus threshold stock.
7. Threshold stock cannot be reserved through normal PO allocation.
8. Accepting a PO creates blocked inventory without reducing physical stock.
9. Concurrent PO acceptance cannot reserve the same units twice.
10. The SCM acceptance email is sent once and a failed send can be retried without duplicating the reservation.
11. One appointment PDF can be automatically matched to the correct PO using the PO ID.
12. Shipment planning remains unavailable until the appointment letter is attached.
13. One PO can be split into multiple shipments with different SKU quantities.
14. Combined shipment quantities cannot exceed blocked PO quantities.
15. Dispatch moves the dispatched quantity from blocked stock to in-transit stock.
16. A POD can confirm delivered, short, damaged and rejected quantities at SKU level.
17. POD confirmation removes only the confirmed delivered quantity from in-transit company stock.
18. Re-uploading the same POD does not duplicate an inventory deduction.
19. The PO becomes Fully Delivered only when all quantities are delivered or covered by an approved short close.
20. Only one invoice can be uploaded for a PO.
21. Invoice upload remains blocked until full delivery or approved short close.
22. QC users can view all permitted operational records but cannot change them.
23. Every required business action produces an immutable audit event.
24. Inventory balances can be rebuilt from inventory movements and match the displayed balances.
25. The complete PO-to-invoice workflow can be completed without an Excel operations tracker.

## 17. Testing requirements

| Layer | Required coverage |

| Unit | PDF field normalisation, warehouse mapping, inventory formulas, threshold enforcement, status transitions and permission checks |

Production release requires all acceptance criteria to pass in user-acceptance testing.

## 18. Delivery plan

### Phase 1: Foundation

- Authentication and roles.
- Warehouses and SKU masters.
- Threshold policies.
- Destination mappings.
- Route estimates.
- Inventory movements and balances.
- Audit framework.

### Phase 2: PO and appointment workflow

- PO PDF upload and extraction.
- Review, duplicate detection, acceptance and rejection.
- Warehouse assignment.
- Inventory blocking and shortages.
- Acceptance email.
- Appointment upload and matching.
- Deadline alerts.

### Phase 3: Shipment and delivery

- Multi-truck shipment planning.
- SKU allocation by truck.
- Dispatch and in-transit inventory.
- Shipment updates.
- POD upload and quantity confirmation.
- Discrepancy and short-close workflow.

### Phase 4: Invoice, dashboard and reports

- One invoice per PO.
- Delivery-based invoice gate.
- Work queues and dashboard.
- Operational reports and exports.
- QC view-only access.
- Backup restoration and go-live checks.

### Sequencing rationale

Inventory movements, thresholds and audit history must be built before PO acceptance. Otherwise, later modules would create stock records that cannot be trusted. PO and appointment handling must precede shipments. Shipment and POD handling must precede invoice gating and final reports.

The product shall not go live until:

- Warehouse and destination mappings are approved.
- Active SKUs exist in the SKU master.
- Threshold quantities are loaded per SKU and warehouse.
- Opening physical inventory is entered and reconciled.
- Roles and permissions are tested.
- Inventory concurrency tests pass.
- At least one real sample PDF from every supported platform is tested.
- A multi-truck PO is completed in user-acceptance testing.
- POD corrections and partial delivery are tested.
- The audit log is verified for all restricted actions.
- A backup is restored successfully into a clean environment.

## 20. Product decisions recorded

1. The tailored operating workflow is the source of truth; the wider SCM/QC PDF provides supporting controls only.
2. Appointments are booked manually on external platforms.
3. PO and appointment PDFs are uploaded sequentially and matched to the PO.
4. The source warehouse is selected automatically from the destination.
5. Only SCM Managers and Administrators may override the warehouse.
6. Stock is blocked when the PO is accepted.
7. Stock becomes in transit when dispatched.
8. Stock leaves company inventory only after POD confirmation.
9. Threshold stock is maintained per SKU per warehouse and cannot be allocated.
10. The SCM team enters stock additions and shipment details.
11. A PO may have multiple trucks and one or more POD files.
12. Each PO has one appointment letter and one invoice.
13. The invoice is created outside the SCM system and uploaded only after full delivery or approved short close.
14. QC has view-only access.
15. Every important action is recorded in an append-only audit log.
