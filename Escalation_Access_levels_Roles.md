# I. Escalation Matrix

## 1. Purchase Order Acceptance
Time till escalation, when a PO is not Accepted.

## 2. Inventory Shortage
Time till escalation, when inventory levels dont match PO requirements.

## 3. Last Shipment Time
Time till escalation, when a shipment is not sent out.

## 4. Shipment POD attachment
Time till escalation, when delivery confirmation or POD is not attached

## 5. Invoice not sent out
Time till escalation, when Invoice is not sent out to vendor.


# II. Access Levels

## Note: 
1. Visibility here means read only access.

## 1. Core SCM team
PO creation, processing, appointment, visibility on inventory, visibility on shipment, visibility on invoice.
Visibility here means read only access.

## 2. Inventory Management Team
Product addition to inventory, inventory management, warehouse management, channel sku mapping, visibility on shipping.

## 3. Shipment Management Team
visibility on inventory, shipment planning, shipment creation, shipment tracking, shipment status, visibility on Invoice.

## 4. Invoicing Team
visibility on shipment state, Invoice attachment and confirmation.

## 5. Administration Team
Admin: Read only access to everything, copilot access.
SuperAdmin: Write and Read access to everything, copilot access.
